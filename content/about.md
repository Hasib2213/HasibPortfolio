Hasibul Islam is a backend engineer and AI-focused developer with a strong foundation in Computer Science and Engineering. He specializes in building scalable web applications and intelligent software solutions using technologies such as Python, Django, FastAPI, React, PostgreSQL, Docker, and modern AI APIs. He has hands-on experience developing RESTful APIs, AI-powered applications, automation workflows, data processing systems, and cloud-based deployments.

With a growing focus on Artificial Intelligence and data-driven technologies, Hasibul actively works on integrating Large Language Models (LLMs), generative AI, image processing, and automation into practical applications. His experience spans projects ranging from food-waste management platforms and AI-powered recommendation systems to automated business review-response systems and AI-driven SaaS solutions.

Based in Bangladesh, Hasibul is passionate about software engineering, AI, data science, and building practical technology solutions that solve real-world problems.
